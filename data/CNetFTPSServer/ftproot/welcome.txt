Welcome to CNetFTPSServer!

This is a sample explicit-FTPS (FTP over TLS) server built entirely on the
CNet library. Everything you see under this folder is served from
data\CNetFTPSServer\ftproot, the server's virtual root ("/").

Try:
  CWD notes
  LIST
  RETR todo.txt
  CDUP

See readme.txt in samples\CNetFTPSServer for connection instructions.
